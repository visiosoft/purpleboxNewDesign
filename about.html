Our story

How we got here.

The problem

Dubai handover dates never line up

You're out on the 30th and into the new place on the 15th. So you hire a mover, then a storage company, then coordinate between two teams who each say the other is responsible when a table leg goes missing.

The idea

Do the whole job with one crew

The people who wrap your sofa should be the people who drive it, the people who store it, and the people who bring it back. One price, one phone number, one team accountable from start to finish.

Launch

Al Quoz Industrial Area 2

We opened with around 300 climate-controlled units across nine sizes, from a 10 sq ft locker to a 200 sq ft villa unit — with Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence and DEWA approvals, and SIRA-connected CCTV throughout.

Growth

Roughly 90% occupancy in three months

Word travelled faster than we expected. Almost all of it came from people who'd used us for a move and stayed for the storage, or told a friend who was in the same handover squeeze.

Now

Packing, moving, storage and supplies

Full packing and moving crews, boxes and materials delivered to your door, and units you can access any hour of the day. All from one facility and one team in Al Quoz.

The facility

Al Quoz Industrial Area 2.

Purpose-fitted for storage rather than a warehouse with partitions thrown up inside it. Everything below applies to every unit, in every size.

01

Climate controlled throughout

AC and humidity control across the whole facility. Dubai summers warp wooden furniture, cloud electronics and yellow documents — indoor storage without climate control doesn't solve that.

02

SIRA-connected CCTV

Cameras across the facility monitored around the clock, meeting Dubai's SIRA security standards. Units are individually locked and you hold the only key.

03

Fully approved

Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence and DEWA approvals in place. Fire detection and suppression throughout the building.

04

24/7 access with lifts

Come and go any hour, any day. Elevator access across the facility so nothing gets carried up stairs, and trolleys available on site.

05

Nine sizes, 10 to 200 sq ft

From a locker for documents to a unit holding an entire villa. Move between sizes as your needs change without changing facility.

06

Loading bay and supplies

Covered loading directly at the building, with boxes, tape, wrap and locks available on site so a forgotten roll of tape doesn't cost you a trip.
