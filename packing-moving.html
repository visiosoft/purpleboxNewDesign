Move day

How the day actually runs.

A typical 2-bedroom apartment move, start to finish. Bigger homes follow the same shape over a longer day.

Before the day — survey

We see what's moving

A walkthrough in person or over WhatsApp video. We count the furniture, check lift access and parking at both ends, and agree the fixed price and the crew size.

08:00 — arrival

Crew arrives and protects the space

Floor runners and door protection go down first. The team leader walks the home with you and confirms what's moving, what's staying, and anything fragile that needs special handling.

08:30 — packing

Everything gets wrapped and boxed

Kitchen and breakables first, then wardrobes, then the rest. Boxes labelled by room as they're sealed. If you've packed some yourself, we check the seals and load them with the rest.

11:00 — disassembly & load

Furniture comes apart and goes in

Beds and wardrobes are broken down, fittings bagged and taped to the frame. Heaviest items load first and get tied down, then boxes fill the gaps.

13:00 — transit

Straight to the new place or to storage

If your new home isn't ready, the truck goes to Al Quoz and everything goes into a climate-controlled unit instead — same team, no second company involved.

14:00 — unload & rebuild

Placed, rebuilt, and set out

Boxes go to the right rooms, furniture is reassembled and positioned where you want it. Beds go back together first so there's somewhere to sleep that night.

16:00 — walkthrough

Final check, packaging cleared

You walk the home with the team leader, flag anything out of place, and we take the used cartons and wrap away with us.
