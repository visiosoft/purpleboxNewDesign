Before you move in

Six things that make storage go smoothly.

✓
Label every box on two sides
Stacked boxes hide their tops. Side labels mean you can read the whole stack without unstacking it.
✓
Leave a walkway down the middle
If you'll visit regularly, plan a corridor to the back wall. Everything is reachable and you won't need a bigger unit.
✓
Disassemble what you can
Bed frames, table legs and shelving flat-pack down dramatically. This alone often drops you a size.
✓
Nothing perishable or flammable
No food, plants, paint, gas cylinders or fuel. Everything else is generally fine — ask us if you're unsure.
✓
Heavy on the bottom, light on top
Books and tools at floor level, linens and lamps above. Fewer crushed boxes, safer to unstack.
✓
Photograph the unit when you finish
One photo of the packed unit tells you months later what's in there and roughly where.
