Packing guide

How the crews do it.

01
Heavy things go in small boxes
Books and tools in small cartons, linens and cushions in large ones. A large box full of books is unliftable and splits at the seams — the most common mistake we see.
02
Fill every gap
Half-empty boxes crush when stacked. Pack to the top and fill voids with paper or soft items so the box holds its shape under weight.
03
Plates stand on edge
Wrapped individually and stacked vertically like records, not flat like a pile. They survive far better under pressure that way.
04
Tape the bottom in an H
One strip along the seam and one across each end. A single strip down the middle is what gives way when you lift a full box.
05
Label two sides, not the top
Stacked boxes hide their lids. Write the room and contents on two sides so you can read the whole stack without taking it apart.
06
Mattresses and sofas need covers
Fabric collects dust and moisture over months in storage. A proper cover costs a fraction of a professional clean later.
